Build it up: tap squares to add or remove hits, or tap ● LAYER and play the drum buttons or sample pads in time. Each hit snaps to the beat and stays in the loop. Hold ROLL for a snare build-up. Set LOOP to 2, 4 or 8 bars for longer patterns: tap the bar numbers to edit each bar, and use COPY → ALL to copy the bar you're on into every bar, then change one bar for a fill.

Every key fits the scale, so whatever you play sounds right — the purple keys are the home note. Each row is one octave above the row below it: the key straight above any key is the same note, higher. Notes lower than the bottom row? Tap − — your tune moves up one row, same columns. Need a note outside the key? Pick CHROMATIC to get all 12 notes. Hold a key to let it ring. With ● LAYER on, your notes snap to the beat and loop (they show on the NOTES row).

🎙 RECORD ON IPHONE

iPhone doesn't let web pages open the voice recorder, and the Claude app blocks the live mic. Use Voice Memos instead:

VOICE MEMOS · AUDIO ONLY
  1. Open Voice Memos and record your take
  2. Tap the recording, then ••• → Share
  3. Scroll the share list down → Save to Files → pick On My iPhone → Save
  4. Come back, tap the button below → Browse → On My iPhone → tap your memo
